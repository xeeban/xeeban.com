# Welcome to xeeban.com

I'm starting this blog as a space to share reflections on decentralized governance, AI, integral theory, and the intersection of technology with what makes us human.

## What to Expect

This space will be a mix of:

- **Learning in Public**: Documenting my 100 Days of AI journey and other explorations
- **Decentralized Collaboration**: Insights from building trust protocols and working with distributed networks
- **Philosophical Reflections**: How Zen practice and integral theory inform organizational design
- **Practical Experiments**: Tools, methods, and frameworks for high-trust collaboration

## Why Now?

We're at an inflection point where AI, blockchain, and new organizational forms are converging. The question isn't just about building new technologies—it's about preserving and enhancing what makes us uniquely human in the process.

## Let's Build Together

This is as much about conversation as it is about content. If something here resonates, reach out. I'm always interested in connecting with people working on similar questions.

More to come soon.
